FAQ

Base vs +sport — straight answers

Do I need Base first?

Yes. Base is required. Sport packs stack on Base. There is no sport-only path and no Base-or-sport exclusive choice.

If I cancel a sport, do I keep Base?

Yes. Cancel a sport pack anytime. Base stays. Packs are independent subscriptions on top of Base.

Can I stack more than one sport?

Yes. Multi-sport stack is the model: Base first, then packs 1→N as you train more than one sport.

Is ROMRx free forever?

No. The assessment can start free. Base and sport packs are paid products. Pricing above is EXAMPLE messaging only — not live checkout.
