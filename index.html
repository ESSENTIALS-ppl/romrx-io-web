The Readiness Protocol™ family

GREEN. YELLOW. RED.

Every sport pack applies your ROM data to that sport's movements. Each movement is rated for your body — right now.

GREEN

Train it.

Your joints clear the ROM thresholds this movement demands.

YELLOW

Modify it.

You're close. Train the modification while you build the missing ROM.

RED

Skip it for now.

The movement requires ROM you don't have yet. Forcing it is where injuries live.

As your ROM improves, GREEN grows and RED shrinks. That's how we know you're getting better — not just feeling better.
