How we partner

Discount codes. Collaborations. And sometimes more.

Track 1

Member discount codes

Give our users a real code, not a fake one. We surface it in the app when your product matches what they're working on. You get intent-matched buyers. They get gear that helps.

Good fit for: recovery hardware, supplements, gear brands, apparel.

Track 2

Content collaborations

Co-produced content: articles, video, protocol integrations. Your expertise, our audience. We only publish what our team believes actually helps a user get results.

Good fit for: coaches, specialists, longevity and diagnostics brands, education-first products.

Track 3

Deeper integrations

Product placement inside a Readiness Protocol, referral flows into partner clinics, hardware integrations that feed ROM data back into the assessment. Custom terms for the right fit.

Good fit for: category leaders, brands with a technology or clinical component, long-term partners.

What we ask of partners

A short list. We hold ourselves to the same standard.

Products you'd give your family

If you wouldn't hand it to your kid, your spouse, or your best training partner, don't send it to our users.

Honest marketing

No made-up studies. No fear-based copy. Claims backed by real evidence or clearly labeled as opinion.

Real customer support

If our users have a problem, a real human at your company answers within a reasonable window. That's the bar.

Skin in the game

The best partnerships aren't transactional. We take partners who want a five-year relationship, not a quarterly campaign.
