Your time-in-zone set the energy and carb/fat mix; lean mass set protein. Macro targets are approximate starting values, not medical prescriptions. Small day-to-day variation is normal.

Formulas & evidence

Lean mass = weight × (1 − body-fat%). Target operating weight = lean mass ÷ (1 − target BF%), target BF 12% men / 22% women.

Zone 0 baseline = 75 × (target wt ÷ 198) kcal/hr. Daily energy = Σ(zone hours × zone kcal/hr). Protein = lean mass(kg) × 1.7 g (fixed). Carbs & fat split the remainder by the zone fuel blend.

These are sweat-replacement estimates to spread across the day via food and drinks, not one forced dose and not your full dietary intake. Rehydration target after a hard session is ~150% of fluid lost.

Formulas & evidence

Zone sweat rates (L/hr): Z1 <0.3 · Z2 0.75 · Z3 1.25 · Z4 2.0 · Z5 2.5. Fluid lost = Σ(zone hours × zone sweat rate). Measured mode overrides with real pre/post weight.

Sodium = fluid lost (L) × Na conc (mmol/L) × 23. Intra-session cap = 400–800 mL/hr (IMMDA). Rehydration = 150% of loss.
