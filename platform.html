ROMRx isn't a workout library. It's an assessment engine that measures your range of motion across 14 joints, identifies the 3 that are holding you back, and prescribes the daily protocol to unlock them — then adapts every 6 weeks.

Step 1

14 joints. 10 minutes. Green / Yellow / Red.

The assessment walks you through active and passive range-of-motion tests for the 14 joints that govern athletic performance — shoulders, thoracic, hips, knees, ankles, and everything connecting them. Each joint gets a score.

GREEN

Full ROM. Ready for anything.

YELLOW

Limited ROM. Modify or work around.

RED

Restricted. Priority to unlock.

Specific joint-to-technique mapping is proprietary and only visible in the sport-pack dashboards.

Step 2

Your Top 3 Priority Joints™.

Not 14 problems. Three. ROMRx picks the joints where fixing them unlocks the most downstream performance — and puts the other 11 on the back burner. This is the anti-overwhelm design decision that makes everything else work.

Step 3

Your daily Readiness Protocol™.

Every day, you get 8–12 exercises. Not 400. The exact strength work, stretching, and foam rolling to move your top 3 joints from red toward green. That's it.

Strength

Load the joint through its available range. Build the capacity that ROM alone won't create.

Stretching

Reclaim range with the right technique for the right joint. Static, dynamic, or PNF — the protocol picks.

Foam Rolling

Tissue prep on the muscles that gate the joint. Not everywhere — only where it moves the needle.

Step 4

ROMBot™ adapts as you improve.

Log a session. Log a soreness. Log a great training day. ROMBot™ adjusts tomorrow's protocol — heavier if you're recovering fast, lighter if you're behind, and shifts emphasis as joints move from red toward green.

Step 5

The 6-Week Reassessment Cycle™.

Every 6 weeks, ROMRx reassesses. Priorities shift. The joint that was red is now yellow — and a joint you didn't know was a problem gets promoted. The protocol never stagnates because your body doesn't.
