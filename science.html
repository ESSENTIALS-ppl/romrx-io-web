ROMRx is opinionated software, but the opinions are load-bearing on decades of published research in range of motion, injury prevention, and athletic performance. Here's the reading list.

  • Hrysomallis C. Hip flexors: three-dimensional anatomy and clinical implications. Sports Medicine. 2007;37(9):781-796.
  • Cook G, Burton L, Hoogenboom BJ, Voight M. Functional movement screening: the use of fundamental movements as an assessment of function. International Journal of Sports Physical Therapy. 2014;9(3):396-409.

  • Bouchard C, Rankinen T. Individual differences in response to regular physical activity. Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise. 2001;33(6 Suppl):S446-51. DOI: 10.1097/00005768-200106001-00013
  • Mann JB, Thyfault JP, Ivey PA, Sayers SP. The effect of autoregulatory progressive resistance exercise vs. linear periodization on strength improvement in college athletes. Journal of Strength & Conditioning Research. 2010;24(7):1718-1723.

Combat Sport & Grappling Demands
