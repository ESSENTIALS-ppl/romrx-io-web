How it works

Three steps from your body to the bar.

STEP 01

Assess in Base.

Run your free Range of Motion assessment. Base identifies your restrictions and asymmetries in about ten minutes.

STEP 02

Build in Base.

Follow your personalized Rx of recommended mobility exercises, and use the hydration and nutrition calculator and ROMBot™ to keep your ROM progressing.

STEP 03

Apply ROM to your lifts.

Add +BodyBuilding in My Sport. Your Base ROM becomes an Exercise Readiness Protocol that maps your body to exercises and lifts.

Exercise Readiness Protocol™

ROMRx+BodyBuilding applies your Base ROM to your lifts.

After you access Base, you add the sport in My Sport. +BodyBuilding produces an Exercise Readiness Protocol that applies your Base ROM to exercises and lifts, so you can see which movements your body is ready for today and which ones to build toward. Train smarter, reduce avoidable injury risk, and keep doing what you love.
