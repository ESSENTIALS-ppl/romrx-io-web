How to Improve Ankle Dorsiflexion for Squatting

By Jim Scott, Founder of ROMRx · Published July 24, 2026 · Updated July 24, 2026

Ankle dorsiflexion is a common squat limiter you can measure objectively. Use the weight-bearing lunge (knee-to-wall test, where roughly 10 cm or more of toe-to-wall is good functional range and a side-to-side difference greater than about 2 to 3 cm is worth noting. Limited dorsiflexion shifts movement strategy up the chain, and range usually improves with loaded mobility work plus temporary tools like a heel lift.
