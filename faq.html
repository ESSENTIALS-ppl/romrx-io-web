How do I start an assessment or reassess (Retest)?

Sign in, then go to My Body.

  • If you have never assessed: you will see No assessment on file and Get started (not Retest). That starts your first assessment.
  • If you already have a saved assessment: use Retest now or Retest early. My Body may also show Retest available now or Next retest: {date}, plus a helper: Retest every 6 weeks to track progress.

Exact path: Sign in → My Body → Get started (first time) or Retest now / Retest early → https://romrx.io/app/onboarding/assessment.

My Body shows a next-retest date about every 6 weeks (42 days from your last assessment). You can also reassess earlier with Retest early when you want an updated snapshot.

Yes. Base beta is free to use through December 31, 2026. Billing starts January 1, 2027. That end date is named on purpose. We will not call it forever-free.

What is Base vs a sport pack?

Base is the general mobility product. Your Base results use everyday bands: Needs focus / Building / Steady. Sport packs (for example BJJ or bodybuilding) are separate add-ons on top of Base. They are not a different “grade” of Base, and Base is not sport Position Readiness.

If you add a sport pack later and cancel only that pack, you keep Base. Start with Base; add a sport pack only if you want that sport layer.
