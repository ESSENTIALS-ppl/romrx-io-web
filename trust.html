Trust and Security

ROMRx is a small company. This page explains, in plain words, how we handle your data and who helps us run ROMRx. For the full rules, see our Privacy Policy.

What we do to protect your data

  • Your connection to romrx.io is encrypted (HTTPS). Our site tells browsers to always use the secure version.
  • Passwords are stored as hashes, never as plain text.
  • Your data is stored in Supabase (a Postgres database) with row-level security turned on.
  • Card details are handled by Stripe. We never see your full card number.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not share your range of motion results, plan or workout data with advertisers.
  • ROMBot chats are deleted after 90 days, or sooner if you delete your account.

No system is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

What we don't claim

We do not hold security certifications such as SOC 2, ISO 27001 or HITRUST. ROMRx is not a medical provider. If that changes, we'll say so here.

Your choices

  • Export your data or delete your account in Settings, or email privacy@romrx.io. We respond within 30 days.
  • Turn off reminder emails in Settings or from the link in any email.
  • Turn off ad sharing with "Don't Sell or Share My Personal Information". We also honor Global Privacy Control where we are required to.

Report a security issue

If you think you found a security problem, email privacy@romrx.io. Please don't access other people's data or disrupt the service while you check.
