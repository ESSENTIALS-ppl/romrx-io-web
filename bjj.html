How it works

Three steps from your body to the mats.

STEP 01

Assess in Base.

Run your free Range of Motion assessment. Base identifies your restrictions and asymmetries in about ten minutes.

STEP 02

Improve in Base.

Follow your personalized Rx of recommended mobility exercises, and use the hydration and nutrition calculator and ROMBot™ to keep your ROM progressing.

STEP 03

Apply ROM to BJJ.

Add +BJJ in My Sport. Your Base ROM becomes a Position Readiness Protocol that maps your body to guard, sweeps, and submissions.

Position Readiness Protocol™

ROMRx+BJJ applies your Base ROM to BJJ techniques.

After you access Base, you add the sport in My Sport. +BJJ produces a Position Readiness Protocol that applies your Base ROM to jiu-jitsu techniques, so you can see which positions your body is ready for today and which ones to build toward.
