Assessment and progress data: ROM joint markers, scores, tiers, session logs, subjective inputs (soreness, energy, and similar), and longitudinal reassessment history so we can show improvement over time.

Engagement data: login and usage events, survey and in-app feedback responses, which sport packs you enable, and subscription or entitlement state.

Billing data: handled by Stripe. We never see your full card details.

2. How we use it

To deliver the service, personalize your protocol, send the emails you signed up for, improve the product, measure our Meta (Facebook and Instagram) ads as described in Who we share it with, and for product research that uses anonymized or aggregated data (including future studies when we publish only non-identifying results). We do not sell your personal data for money.

3. Who we share it with

We use service providers necessary to run the service: Supabase (database), Netlify (hosting), Stripe (billing), Resend (transactional email), and Google Workspace (support inbox).

For measurement of our Meta (Facebook and Instagram) ads, we may share limited event and identifier data with Meta Platforms, Inc. That can include browser or device signals (such as page URL, IP address, and browser type), cookie or click identifiers (such as _fbp and _fbc when present), hashed contact information you already gave us (for example email), and conversion events (such as page view or signup). We send some of those events from your browser (Meta Pixel) and some from our servers (Conversions API).

We do not sell your personal data for money. The Meta measurement sharing described above may be treated as "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising under California and similar state privacy laws. You can opt out as described in Your rights.

We do not share your assessment ROM scores, protocol details, or other health-adjacent workout data with Meta for ads.
