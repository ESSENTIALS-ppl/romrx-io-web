Privacy Policy

1. What we collect

Account data: name, email, password hash, and demographics you choose to provide (for example age and sex or gender, and related height/weight ranges when collected).

Assessment and progress data: ROM joint markers, scores, tiers, session logs, and reassessment history so we can show improvement over time.

Engagement data: login and usage events, survey and in-app feedback responses, which sport packs you enable, and subscription or entitlement state.

ROMBot chats: if you are signed in, we save your ROMBot messages and ROMBot's replies so you can see your chat history. We delete them after 90 days, or sooner if you delete your account. If you are not signed in, we do not save them.

Billing data: handled by Stripe. We never see your full card details.

2. How we use it

To deliver the service, personalize your protocol, send the emails you signed up for, improve the product, measure our Meta (Facebook and Instagram) ads as described in Who we share it with, and for product research that uses anonymized or aggregated data (including future studies when we publish only non-identifying results). We do not sell your personal data.

3. Who we share it with

We use service providers necessary to run the service: Supabase (database), Netlify (hosting), Stripe (billing), Resend (transactional email), Google Workspace (support inbox), and OpenAI (to generate ROMBot answers).

When you use ROMBot, we send your message, recent messages in that chat, and parts of your ROMRx profile and results (such as your first and last name, readiness levels, focus areas, and plan) to OpenAI to create an answer. If you use ROMBot as a coach, we also send information about your active athletes (such as their names, belts, readiness levels, and priority joints) so ROMBot can answer questions about your team. We set our requests so OpenAI does not store them as saved conversations. OpenAI's API terms say it does not use this data to train its models by default. We do not send your age, gender, or injury notes to ROMBot.

For measurement of our Meta (Facebook and Instagram) ads, we may share limited event and identifier data with Meta Platforms, Inc. That can include browser or device signals (such as page URL, IP address, and browser type), cookie or click identifiers (such as _fbp and _fbc when present), and conversion events (such as page view or signup). We send some of those events from your browser (Meta Pixel) and some from our servers (Conversions API).

We do not sell your personal data. The Meta measurement sharing described above may be treated as "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising under California and similar state privacy laws. You can opt out as described in Your rights.

We do not share your assessment ROM scores, protocol details, or other health-adjacent workout data with Meta for ads.

You can request a full export of your data, or full deletion, by emailing privacy@romrx.io. We respond within 30 days. When you ask us to delete your data, we delete your account and the personal information tied to you. Data that has already been anonymized or combined so it no longer identifies you, such as group statistics in past reports, may be kept.

We do not sell your personal information. Sometimes we use limited data so people who can really use ROMRx can find us, including measuring our ads. California privacy law may call that a "sale" or "share." The Don't Sell or Share My Personal Information control turns those uses off. Prefer email? When ads measurement is allowed under Your rights (and, for EU or UK visitors, after required consent), we also use Meta Pixel cookies and related identifiers for advertising measurement.
